# The Framework.

## A repeatable playbook for AI adoption

---

## Three steps to deploy AI that sticks.

### 1 — Identify the bottleneck

Map your team's workflow. Find the task that's high-volume, rule-based, and draining. That's your entry point — not the flashiest use case, the most painful one.

### 2 — Pilot with guardrails

Start with one team, one workflow, one measurable outcome. Set a 30-day window. Human review stays in the loop. No big-bang rollouts.

### 3 — Measure and scale

Track the metric that matters: time saved, error reduction, throughput. If the pilot moves the needle, expand. If it doesn't, pivot — fast.

## What we've learned.

- **Start small, prove fast** — one workflow, one team, 30 days
- **Measure what matters** — time saved is the universal metric
- **Keep humans in the loop** — AI drafts, humans decide
- **Train the workflow, not the tool** — process change > software change
- **Share wins loudly** — adoption spreads through stories, not mandates

> "Every hour you save someone is an hour they can spend on work that actually matters."
