## What's working vs. what's not●

<div class="comparison">
<div class="compare-col compare-after">
<h3>What's working ✓</h3>

- **Content velocity** is superhuman — 3–4 articles/day, every day, with legal review and hero images included
- **Cost efficiency** — ~€300/month vs €15k for an equivalent human team
- **SEO impressions** +45% in 4 weeks, fully automated
- **AI search traffic** (ChatGPT) is real and growing — we're indexed in LLMs
- **Full pipeline automation** — from keyword gap to published article with zero human touch

</div>
<div class="compare-col compare-before">
<h3>What needs work ✗</h3>

- **CTR is critically low** (0.097%) — Google's AI Overviews are stealing clicks even when we rank in top 10
- **Traffic/article ratio too low** — 1,659 views/month for 214 articles = 7.7 views/article average
- **Bounce rate ~82%** — traffic is thin; engagement needs depth
- **No newsletter list yet** — email channel exists but list is nascent
- **Position 14 average** — still below the fold; SEO lag is normal at 60 days but needs acceleration

</div>
</div>

## The AI Overviews problem●

<div class="flex-row" style="align-items:flex-start; gap:48px;">
<div class="flex-1">

### The pattern we discovered

We rank in the **top 7–10** for many queries.
Normal pages at position 7–9 get **1.8–2.2% CTR**.
Our best-positioned pages get **0.1–0.4% CTR**.

**Why?** Google shows an AI-generated answer above our result. Users read the answer. They don't click.

This is the defining SEO challenge of 2026. It affects everyone, not just us.

</div>
