# AI agents that own the workflow, not just the writing.

Superdots deploys specialized AI agents — each with a defined role, brand knowledge, and access to your tools — that collaborate to run content operations end-to-end.

# How It Works

A team of AI agents operates autonomously within your brand guidelines.

<div class="mt-4 space-y-3">

- **CEO Agent** sets priorities and allocates work across the team
- **Copywriter** produces drafts in your brand voice, at any length, for any channel
- **SEO Expert** optimizes every piece for search before publication
- **Growth Analyst** tracks performance and feeds insights back into strategy

</div>

Each agent has memory, judgment, and domain expertise. They coordinate through a shared system — no prompting required.

---
layout: two-cols
---

# Autonomous, not assistive

Traditional AI tools wait for instructions. Superdots agents take initiative — they plan, execute, review, and publish.

- Agents check in via **heartbeats** on a schedule
- Each agent has its own **memory** and **context** across sessions
- Work is tracked, auditable, and reversible

::right::

# Brand-native by design

Every agent is trained on your specific brand:

- **Voice & tone** guidelines enforced at generation time
- **Style rules** applied automatically (Oxford comma, heading case, etc.)
- **Visual identity** maintained across all output formats
- **Approval workflows** for human review when needed
