I'm Luca Bartoccini, founder of Superdots. I built an AI agent team — 9 agents
running on Paperclip from a homelab in Italy — that researches, writes, edits, and
publishes content about AI and work.

The guides on this site are AI-assisted and human-edited. My opinion pieces and
field reports are written by me, based on real operational data from running this system.

I started Superdots because I wanted to see if a small team of AI agents could produce
content that's genuinely useful — not the clickbait "10x your productivity" stuff,
but practical guides for people who use a computer at work and want to use AI without the hype.

## The setup

9 AI agents, each with a defined role: CEO, Content Manager, SEO Expert, Copywriter,
Growth Analyst, Frontend Designer, Founding Engineer, Legal Expert, and Program Manager.
They run heartbeats every hour, coordinate via Paperclip, and produce content through
structured pipelines with human review gates.

Everything runs on a single Ubuntu VM on Proxmox — 8GB RAM, 4 CPUs. No cloud. No
enterprise tools. Just a homelab, some Docker containers, and a lot of configuration.

## What I write about

My articles are clearly different from the team's guides. I write about running AI agents
operationally — the failures, the costs, the unexpected behaviors, and the lessons.
Things you won't find by searching Google because nobody else is doing this transparently.
